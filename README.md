# INTRODUCTION

**Some worlds are discovered. Others are awakened.**

Eclyria: Riftfall is an independent fantasy RPG project created for Minecraft, inspired by the depth and atmosphere of MMORPG experiences.

The project explores the idea of transforming the familiar Minecraft experience into a larger fantasy adventure built around exploration, character identity, magical progression, unique equipment, mysterious dimensions, and extraordinary creatures.

Eclyria takes place in a world shaped by ancient forces and forgotten histories. Beyond familiar lands exist dimensions that hold unfamiliar environments, mysterious resources, and creatures that challenge the boundaries of the known world.

Players are envisioned as adventurers entering a world where discovery and progression are closely connected.

From choosing a character class to exploring the Rift dimensions, collecting rare artifacts, and encountering powerful creatures, every part of Eclyria contributes to a larger fantasy experience.

**Eclyria: Riftfall is an entirely original project conceived and developed by Darknet_Dweller.**

---

# THE VISION

The central ambition behind Eclyria is to create a Minecraft RPG that feels like an independent fantasy world rather than a collection of unrelated modifications.

The project is built around several creative pillars.

### A World Worth Exploring

Every dimension should have its own atmosphere, visual identity, resources, inhabitants, and sense of discovery.

### Meaningful Character Identity

Character classes should influence how players experience the world, allowing different combat identities and approaches to progression.

### A Distinctive Fantasy Identity

From magical artifacts to mysterious creatures, the project aims to establish a recognizable visual and thematic identity.

### Exploration and Discovery

The world should reward curiosity. Ancient ruins, unusual environments, rare materials, and unfamiliar creatures contribute to the sense of adventure.

### Connected Gameplay Systems

Progression, equipment, magic, exploration, and character identity are designed to complement one another as parts of a larger experience.

---

# THE WORLD OF ECLYRIA

Eclyria is envisioned as a world containing distinct regions, forgotten places, mysterious powers, and dimensions beyond ordinary understanding.

Its identity is built around the contrast between familiar fantasy environments and strange realities beyond them.

The world is not simply a backdrop for combat. It is intended to be an important part of the experience itself.

Its environments, creatures, resources, and locations contribute to the atmosphere and identity of each adventure.

## THE RIFT

The Rift represents the mysterious dimensional aspect of Eclyria.

It connects the familiar world to environments that exist beyond ordinary boundaries.

Each Rift dimension possesses its own identity, with different landscapes, resources, creatures, and exploration opportunities.

Three major dimensions form the foundation of the current project vision.

---

# THE RIFT DIMENSIONS

## I. VERDANT REACH

<sub>THE DOMAIN OF ANCIENT NATURE</sub>

*Where nature remembers what civilization has forgotten.*

Verdant Reach is a nature-inspired dimension shaped by ancient growth, forgotten places, and mysterious natural forces.

Its atmosphere is built around the contrast between beauty and danger. The environment represents a world where nature has continued to evolve beyond the influence of ordinary civilization.

**Environmental identity**

- Lush and overgrown landscapes.
- Ancient natural surroundings.
- Forgotten ruins and structures.
- Distinctive regional resources.
- Creatures associated with nature.
- Exploration through unfamiliar environments.

**Regional materials**

The dimension features resources such as:

- Verdantite
- Duskcoal
- Stormsteel

These materials contribute to the identity of the region and its equipment themes.

## II. EMBER RIFT

<sub>THE DOMAIN OF ASH AND FIRE</sub>

*Where fire consumes the old and creates the new.*

Ember Rift is a hostile dimension associated with volcanic environments, heat, ash, and ancient remnants.

Its identity is built around harsh landscapes and the presence of dangerous inhabitants.

The dimension represents an environment where exploration requires preparation and awareness.

**Environmental identity**

- Volcanic landscapes.
- Ash-covered surroundings.
- Distinctive mineral deposits.
- Ancient remnants.
- Hostile creatures.
- An atmosphere shaped by fire and destruction.

**Regional materials**

Its resources include:

- Emberstone
- Soulcopper
- Suniron

These materials establish a distinctive identity for Ember-themed equipment and progression.

## III. VOID RIFT

<sub>THE DOMAIN BEYOND REALITY</sub>

*Beyond reality lies something yet to be understood.*

Void Rift is a mysterious dimension associated with unfamiliar environments, strange energies, and the unknown.

Unlike the natural landscapes of Verdant Reach or the hostile volcanic atmosphere of Ember Rift, the Void represents something more difficult to understand.

It is intended to feel unfamiliar, mysterious, and distinctly separate from ordinary Minecraft environments.

**Environmental identity**

- Unfamiliar landscapes.
- Distinctive atmospheric environments.
- Rare dimensional resources.
- Unusual creatures.
- Mysterious discoveries.
- A strong sense of isolation and exploration.

**Regional materials**

Its resources include:

- Voidsilver
- Frostquartz
- Aetherium
- Riftglass

# CHARACTER CLASSES

Character identity is one of the foundations of Eclyria.

The project features four distinct character classes, each representing a different approach to combat and adventure.

## VANGUARD

<sub>THE UNBREAKABLE</sub>

The Vanguard represents resilience, strength, and frontline combat.

Designed around the identity of a traditional warrior, the class focuses on standing against dangerous enemies and maintaining a strong presence in combat.

**Class identity**

- Frontline combat.
- Defensive specialization.
- Close-range encounters.
- Strength-oriented gameplay.

## ARCANIST

<sub>THE ARCANE SCHOLAR</sub>

The Arcanist represents the study and manipulation of magical forces.

Its identity revolves around arcane power, magical abilities, and the use of mana to influence combat.

**Class identity**

- Arcane magic.
- Magical offense.
- Mana-oriented gameplay.
- Ability-focused combat.

## RANGER

<sub>THE PATHFINDER</sub>

The Ranger represents precision, awareness, and adaptability.

A class associated with ranged combat and mobility, designed around maintaining distance and responding to changing encounters.

**Class identity**

- Precision combat.
- Ranged specialization.
- Mobility.
- Tactical encounters.

## LUMINARY

<sub>THE DIVINE WIELDER</sub>

The Luminary represents radiant powers and divine influence.

Its identity is associated with restoration, magical support, and the spiritual side of Eclyria.

**Class identity**

- Radiant magic.
- Magical support.
- Restoration.
- Divine themes.

<sub>THE DIVINE PATH</sub>

This system contributes to the wider mythology and creates another dimension to character development beyond ordinary equipment and combat.

# HAVENFALL

<sub>THE SANCTUARY BETWEEN ADVENTURES</sub>

*A place to begin. A place to return.*

Havenfall is envisioned as the central sanctuary of Eclyria.

Havenfall serves as the conceptual home of the adventurer, providing a connection to the wider world and establishing the starting identity of the experience.

It is an important part of the project's worldbuilding and overall atmosphere.
